# HunterMage

## About the Game

HunterMage is a top-down action game where you play as a mage, using point-and-click controls to cast spells and a keyboard to move and switch between spells.

Defeated enemies can be harvested for materials, which can be used to craft and improve potions and spells.

## Gameplay Features

### Magic and Combat
- Cast spells using point-and-click controls.
- Move around the world using the keyboard.
- Switch between spells to adapt to different situations.
- Defeat enemies and harvest materials from their remains.
- Use harvested materials to create and upgrade potions and spells.

### Factions and Reputation

The world contains various factions, each with its own attitude toward the player. Your actions influence how different factions perceive and treat you.

Faction relationships may be influenced by:

- **Combat:** Defeat a faction's enemies to earn its favor.
- **Trading and Supplies:** Gain favor by feeding factions or providing them with supplies.
- **Good Deeds:** Improve relationships by acting in a good or helpful manner.
- **Evil Deeds:** Gain favor with factions that approve of evil actions.
- **Neutrality:** Some factions can never become truly friendly.
- **Hostility:** Some factions are always aggressive toward the player.

### Achievements

Players can earn achievements by reaching milestones and discovering new things throughout the game.

Planned achievement categories include:

- **Faction Reputation:** Reach specific friendship or reputation levels with factions.
- **Boss Hunting:** Defeat powerful bosses.
- **Spell Discovery:** Discover new spells.
- **Potion Discovery:** Discover new potions.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Explore the world and encounter enemies.
2. Fight enemies using spells.
3. Harvest materials from defeated enemies.
4. Craft and improve spells and potions.
5. Interact with factions and influence their attitudes toward you.
6. Discover new abilities, defeat bosses, and unlock achievements.

## Project Status

HunterMage is a game concept focused on spell-based combat, resource gathering, crafting, faction relationships, and exploration.
